# Try NextKnock with fictional material

This demo needs no accounts, personal documents, or external actions. All candidate and company material is fictional. It illustrates the workflow and does not demonstrate live platform execution.

## 1. Explore a direction

After installing NextKnock, attach or point your agent to [sample-cv.md](sample-cv.md) and try:

```text
Use $nextknock for an offline demo with the fictional Jordan Vale CV. I want more project ownership and less ticket-queue work. I have no portfolio and cannot share employer documents. Compare sensible career directions and explain what evidence I would need for each. Do not access accounts or apply. Keep demo outputs in a separate temporary workspace.
```

An illustrative comparison:

| Direction | Relevant evidence | Gap or tradeoff | Useful exploration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Support enablement | Wrote help articles; documented recurring questions. | No proven ownership of an enablement program; some roles retain queue duties. | Inspect role responsibilities and write a self-initiated sample guide. |
| Implementation coordinator | Maintained onboarding checklist; documented handoff issues. | Helped an implementation team but did not own customer implementations. | Review real coordinator roles and ask a practitioner about typical ownership. |
| Implementation project manager | Customer communication and process documentation transfer. | End-to-end delivery, scope, and dependency management are not established. | Explore a small supervised project before claiming delivery leadership. |

These are examples, not guaranteed recommendations or verified live vacancies. The actual agent should discuss Jordan's priorities and remaining constraints before pursuing a direction.

## 2. Explore an outreach angle

Provide [research-packet.md](research-packet.md) and try:

```text
Continue the offline demo. Using only the fictional research packet, draft a local exploratory message to Sam. My voice is direct, warm, and understated. Explain the connection between the product detail and Jordan's actual experience. Do not invent a vacancy, contact address, product usage, or impact metric. Do not save anything to Gmail.
```

The useful connection is between saved checklists and Jordan's handoff work. The office announcement is recent but has no demonstrated relevance. The agent should label the output as fictional, provide a message for local review, and avoid any claim of live verification. See [annotated outreach examples](../references/outreach-examples.md) for editing choices.

## 3. Prepare for a response

```text
In this fictional exercise, Sam replied: “Happy to chat. Could you send an example and suggest a time?” Jordan's employer documents are confidential and availability is not yet confirmed. Prepare a local reply and a short call brief. Keep missing information visible; do not promise a sample, schedule a meeting, or imply the conversation already happened.
```

Expect a reply that acknowledges the request, proposes a truthful alternative to confidential work, and asks for the user's availability before finalizing times. A call brief can prepare relevant stories and questions without inventing commitments.
