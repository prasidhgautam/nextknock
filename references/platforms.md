# Platform profiles, applications, and manual intervention

## Supported access

Inspect available connectors and their documentation first. For browser work, use the host's documented browser/UI tools, current observations, and observed URLs/IDs. Follow any required initialization sequence. Do not use hidden endpoints, cookie extraction, injected scripts, unofficial scraping, or CAPTCHA bypasses. An unavailable connector is a reason to offer supported browser or manual steps, not pretend the account action succeeded.

Identify the account before reading or writing. Use the user's chosen job-search identity; do not assume the active account is appropriate. Limit mail access to relevant career threads. If access is missing, provide connection/sign-in instructions grounded in the available interface and continue research or drafting locally.

LinkedIn has a specific permission boundary: ordinary browser automation of its website is prohibited by its published guidance. Use [LinkedIn assistance](linkedin.md) for manual Easy Apply, invitations and messages, or a verified explicitly permitted integration for the exact operation. Browser control, user consent, and Premium alone do not establish platform permission. External employer forms retain their own supported workflow.

## Profiles and missing accounts

If a profile exists, compare it with the approved candidate evidence. Draft accurate headlines, summaries, experience descriptions, skills, and work-sample links suited to the agreed direction. Show the proposed changes and publish only the authorized fields. Do not rewrite a CV file unless requested.

If no account exists, explain the setup steps and prepare profile content. Assist through supported controls when requested, leaving passwords, verification codes, identity checks, account terms, and personal declarations for the user. Do not create duplicate accounts or purchase subscriptions. Resume after the user completes the required step and verify the actual account/profile state.

## Apply selectively

Read the full vacancy and official company/careers information. Verify that the opening is current, the employer or recruiting partner is credible, and the responsibilities fit the search brief. Record missing compensation or reporting information as unknown. Do not manufacture a company problem to justify the candidate's relevance.

Use the [evidence bank](evidence-and-voice.md) to connect the important responsibilities to supported examples. Explain strong fit, plausible stretch, or skip in plain language. Check firm constraints first; a preferred industry is different from a work-authorization requirement. Keep unknowns visible and do not assign artificial fit percentages.

Use the approved document version. Tailor answers and notes to the role, while preserving the source and scope of claims. A listed skill alone does not establish years of experience. Answer screening filters literally. Prepare the rest of a form when a required fact is unknown, then ask for the missing information in one concise bundle. Optional unanswered fields may stay blank.

Persist `submitting` before final submission. Observe a success screen or application record before recording `applied`. A saved form, uploaded CV, or drafted email is not a submitted application. If submission is uncertain, use `needs_verification` and inspect the record before retrying. Never resubmit blindly.

## Manual intervention

When an upload, sign-in, permissions prompt, unsupported control, or verification challenge blocks progress:

1. Record the affected opportunity, platform/account, actual page/reference, completed steps, and the blocker.
2. Explain the exact action the user must complete, the intended document if relevant, and the observed result needed to resume. Do not invent controls you cannot see.
3. Mark `needs_manual_intervention`; keep prepared work and continue independent opportunities.
4. When the user returns, inspect the current state. Confirm whether upload or submission occurred, then resume from that state rather than repeating the action.

A confirmed failed operation can be resumed. An uncertain operation must first be verified. Escalate a persistent platform restriction to manual completion instead of repeatedly retrying it.
